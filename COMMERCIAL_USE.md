# Commercial Use Notice

TACTIVS may be used for commercial purposes only after a written notice is
sent under Section 3 of the [license](LICENSE).

Send the notice to `lin.chen@ed.ac.uk` with the subject:

```text
TACTIVS Commercial Use Notice
```

Include the legal entity name, contact details, a concise description of the
commercial use, and its expected start date. Keep a copy of the sent notice for
your records. Approval or a reply is not required, but notice must be sent
before commercial use begins.
